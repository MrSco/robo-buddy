Basics

Getting Started & Desktop Furniture

Robo Buddy is an interactive companion who lives on your Windows taskbar. He reacts to your mouse, music, open applications, and physics.

  • Poking & Dragging: Click him to poke. Hold and drag to carry him by an arm or leg. Fling him with the mouse to throw him across your desktop.
  • Windows are Furniture: Drop him on another window's title bar and he stands on it. Drop him just short and he grabs the edge and pulls himself up. When left alone, he strolls along title bars and hops between windows.
  • Tray & Context Menu: Right-click him or his tray icon to access Settings, bring him to your active screen, pause reactions, or quit.
  • Double-Click: Opens the talk box to chat (when Talk is enabled).
Context

Clippy Mode (Proactive Companion)

Buddy watches your active workspace and chimes in with clever quips, contextual tips, and interactive action chips.

  • App Awareness: Recognizes when you are coding in VS Code, researching on Chrome/Edge/Firefox, drafting in Office/Notion, 3D modeling in Blender, or pulling a late-nighter (1 AM – 5 AM).
  • Action Chips: Clickable chips in his speech bubble (like [Regex Cheat-sheet], [Explain Code], or [Focus Mode]) open pre-filled AI chats or mute apps.
  • 100% Local Privacy: Process inspection runs entirely on your PC. Password managers (1Password, Bitwarden, KeePass) and private/incognito windows are automatically ignored. Window titles are never sent to external AI without your explicit consent.
  • Anti-Annoyance: Requires 18+ seconds of continuous app focus before triggering (no Alt-Tab interruptions) and pauses while you are AFK.
Physics

Window Physics & Climbing

Fine-tune how your companion physically interacts with the Windows desktop and open application windows.

  • Gravity & Throwing: Adjust Gravity, Bounciness, and Throw strength in the Reactions tab. Flinging him creates realistic airborne tumbling and ragdoll landings.
  • Surface Climbing: Enable Stand on and climb other windows so he roams open title bars, climbs up window stacks, and ducks underneath top screen borders.
  • Click-through Precision: On the Window tab, choose Precise so transparent parts of his window pass clicks through to apps underneath, or Locked to make him completely non-clickable.
  • Taskbar Overlap: Toggle Stand on the taskbar to let his feet rest directly on the Windows taskbar edge.
Audio

Music Detection & Dancing

Buddy dances to whatever audio plays through your speakers or headphones using low-latency WASAPI loopback.

  • Private & Local: Audio is analyzed purely for frequency peaks and rhythmic tempo. Nothing is recorded, stored, or sent over the network.
  • Beat Lock: Controls how quickly he commits to a beat. Higher values react instantly to drums; lower values ensure the beat holds steady for several seconds first, filtering out speech, podcasts, and game gunfire.
  • BPM Meter: The live meter in the Reactions tab displays the detected tempo (BPM) in real time.
  • Custom Dances: Choose between a specific dance, random shuffle, or the procedural Groove dance that works on every humanoid model.
AI & Voice

Talk, LLM Providers & Piper Voices

Bring your companion to life with conversational AI, speech recognition, and high-quality local neural voices.

  • Two-Minute Setup: Enable Let him talk on the Talk tab. Choose Groq (free, blazing-fast key at console.groq.com/keys), Gemini (free at aistudio.google.com/apikey), or Ollama (100% offline local model).
  • Secure Storage: API keys are stored in Windows Credential Manager, never written to disk.
  • Piper Neural Voices: Install Piper (22 MB download) for completely offline, zero-latency neural TTS. Drop any compatible .onnx voice pair into the voices folder.
  • GPT-Live Voice: Select Live Voice engine with an OpenAI key for real-time full-duplex spoken conversations where you can naturally interrupt him.
  • Voice & Chat Commands: Tell him "dance", "do the chicken dance", "sleep", "wake up", "be quiet for 15 minutes", "climb", or "jump".
  • System Hotkey: Set a global shortcut (e.g. Ctrl+Alt+Space) on the Talk tab to instantly toggle the talk box from any application.
Screensaver

Screensaver & Desktop Havoc

Turn your idle desktop into an interactive playground of controlled chaos.

  • Desktop Snapshot: Right-click him and choose Screensaver now, or set an idle timer. Buddy takes a screenshot of your screen to play on—your actual windows and desktop files are never touched.
  • Destruction Styles: Choose between Falling grid tiles and Cracked glass cutouts. Buddy leaps around smashing pieces while ambient erosion fractures the screen.
  • CRT Power-Off & Void: Simulates a vintage CRT monitor collapsing into a glowing phosphor dot and black void.
  • Windows Saver Integration: Seamlessly hands off to your normal Windows screensaver (.scr) or returns instantly to your desktop on mouse movement or keypress.
Characters

Custom Characters & Webcam Mocap

Import your own 3D avatars, 2D sprites, and record custom animations with your webcam.

  • 3D Rig Imports: Drop any GLB, glTF, VRM, or humanoid FBX file. Mixamo, Unreal, and VRoid skeletons are automatically recognized and retargeted; every animation works on every model.
  • Create an Avatar of Yourself: Use a phone 3D scan (e.g., in3D), auto-rig it on mixamo.com, and drop the exported GLB/FBX directly onto Buddy.
  • 2D Sprites: Drop an animated WebP, GIF, or PNG to turn Buddy into a flat pixel companion.
  • Copy Me (Webcam Mocap): On the Capture tab (or right-click → Copy me), your webcam tracks your posture and 21 hand points via local MediaPipe. Tick Mirror on the buddy to copy you live, and record custom clips directly into your animation library.
Diagnostics

Troubleshooting & Tips

Quick solutions to common questions and system behaviors.

  • Buddy wandered off or vanished: Right-click the system tray icon and select Bring buddy here to teleport him directly onto your active monitor. Note: He automatically hides while a fullscreen game or video player is active.
  • False dancing: If game audio or podcasts trigger dancing, lower the Beat lock slider so steady rhythm is required before he grooves.
  • Talk connection errors: Click the Test button next to the API key in the Talk tab to view the provider's exact error message. Ensure model names are up to date.
  • Audio loopback mute: If speaker levels read zero in the meter, ensure your Windows default playback device is active and not exclusively claimed by another application.
  • Diagnostic Logs: When reporting a bug or glitch, check %APPDATA%\com.rocco.robobuddy\buddy.log for state traces and animation warnings.
