Getting started

Robo Buddy is a little character who lives on your taskbar. He watches the mouse, dances to your music, and can be picked up, thrown, poked and talked to.

  • Click him to poke. Hold and drag to carry him around; grab an arm or a leg and he hangs from it. Fling him and he flies.
  • Windows are furniture. Drop him on a title bar and he stands on it; drop him a bit short and he grabs the edge and pulls himself up. Left alone he climbs nearby windows and hops back down.
  • Double-click to talk (once Talk is on). Right-click for the menu; the tray icon has the same one.
  • Every setting applies immediately. Hover any option for a short explanation.

Characters

Bundled: Rocco (a 3D scan), Rocco as VRM, a mannequin and a 2D Pixel Pal. Add your own with Import model… on the Character tab or by dropping a file onto the buddy.

  • 3D: GLB, glTF, VRM or FBX with a humanoid skeleton. Mixamo, Unreal and VRM bone names are recognised, so a free rig from Mixamo or a VRoid character works out of the box.
  • Make one of yourself: a phone scan from in3D (or similar) exported as GLB, auto-rigged on mixamo.com, downloaded as FBX or GLB.
  • 2D: an animated WebP, GIF or PNG becomes a flat buddy.
  • Every animation in the library works on every 3D character; nothing is baked per model.
  • Managing your stuff: imported characters get a "Remove" button under the preview, your own clips a 🗑 on the Animations tab, voices a Delete next to the voice list, and personalities an editor with Delete. Built-in characters, clips and profiles cannot be removed; switch clips to "not used" instead. Every tab has a Folder… button to the files.

Capture: be the animation

The Capture tab (or right-click him and choose Copy me) turns your webcam on and tracks your body on your PC; nothing is uploaded. Tick Mirror on the buddy and he copies you live. Press Record, move, press Stop, give it a name and a role, and it lands in your animations, working on every 3D character. Stand back so your whole body is in frame. The camera turns off when you leave the tab or close this window.

Animations

The Animations tab lists the bundled Mixamo and Quaternius clips plus your own. Give each clip a role (idle, fidget, dance, poke, held, fall, walk) or switch it off. Press ▶ to preview it on the selected character. Drop FBX or GLB animations onto the page or the buddy to add more; Mixamo exports work directly.

Talking: two-minute setup

  1. Turn on Let him talk on the Talk tab.
  2. Pick a provider. Groq is free and fast: create a key at console.groq.com/keys. Gemini is free too: aistudio.google.com/apikey. Ollama needs no key: install it, pull a model, choose Ollama.
  3. Paste the key and press Save key. It lands in Windows Credential Manager, never in a file.
  4. Press ↻ next to Chat model and pick one, or keep the default. Press Test.
  5. Double-click him and say hello. The ☰ button on the strip shows what you both said this session.
  6. He takes orders too: "dance", "do the chicken dance for a minute", "stop", "go to sleep", "wake up", "come here", "jump", "climb", "walk left", "be quiet for 20 minutes", or "do the interact" for any clip he has.

Personality decides who he is: the persona sent to the model, the lines he says on his own, and how creative he is. Copy a built-in profile to make your own. With Generate fresh speech-bubble lines on, he writes himself new idle remarks once a week.

Microphone

The mic button records until you click it again and sends the clip to the chat endpoint for transcription. Groq's whisper-large-v3-turbo is the default. To keep audio on this PC, focus the chat input and press Windows + H for Windows voice typing instead.

Voices

Windows voice works with no setup. Piper is a neural voice that runs on your PC:

  1. Choose Piper as the engine and press Install Piper (a 22 MB download into the app's data folder).
  2. Download a voice from the catalogue, or press Folder… and drop in any Piper voice: a pair of name.onnx and name.onnx.json. Cloned voices trained for Piper work the same way.
  3. Pick it in the Voice list. Replies are spoken with it and his head nods along.

If something is off

  • Can't see him: right-click the tray icon and choose Bring buddy here. He hides while a fullscreen app is focused.
  • He dances to game audio: lower the beat lock, so a tempo has to hold steady for longer before he believes it.
  • He won't dance to music: raise the beat lock, and check the meter is reading a sensible tempo while a song plays.
  • Live voice: on the Talk tab, Engine → Live voice needs an OpenAI key. The talk box then opens a real-time voice session: just speak; the mic button mutes. It ends when the box closes or after 45 quiet seconds, and stops for the day at the minutes cap.
  • Talk fails: the Test button shows the provider's exact error. Most often it is a wrong key or a model id the provider no longer offers; press ↻ to see the current list.
  • Report a glitch: %APPDATA%\com.rocco.robobuddy\buddy.log records odd animation frames with the state they happened in.
